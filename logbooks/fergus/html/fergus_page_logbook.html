Friday, June 12th, 2026
  • Fixed broken electrical connection on elevator controller CAN buss landing board (with LCD screen). A mechanically unsupported, straight connector was used when the milled PCB was fabricated, and the electrical contact pins were bearing the full weight of the DB9 connector and cable. The connector got bumped last week and most all the pins on the board-edge-side snapped off. I moved the last good pin on that side to the in-use CAN line that became disconnected and recovered elevator motor function. Poor design. A right-angle female DB9 connector with mechanical support is recommended (bolt it to the board).
